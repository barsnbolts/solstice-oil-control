02 / What moves the design forward

Measure the interfaces first.

Read the measurement & bench plan ↗
1

Identify and register the pan

Record the actual pan, cover and screws. Establish repeatable datums and the transform to installed car axes.

2

Measure one complete fit envelope

Capture all five seats, original bolt stack, cover underside, floor sections, cast obstacles and the full pickup/tube envelope.

3

Trial-fit, then compare

Use a complete disposable mock-up. Measure real purchased gates or qualify a custom hinge coupon before the full metal assembly.
