02 / Saved geometry · digital scenarios

Where each concept clears — and where it touches.

Full pose, overlap and path data ↓

@@FIT_SUMMARY@@

Each card shows exact Boolean intersections between saved CAD and its named proxy context. Moving doors are sampled every 5° from closed to 60°, plus the nominal roof-critical angle. Stress values are deliberate sensitivity choices, not measured pan ranges.

The digital fit assessment includes the gravity-direction torque sweep, withdrawal results and their limits.

@@FIT_SCENARIOS@@

How much can each proxy change before a sampled intersection?

Each scan changes one proxy value at a time, holds insert placement fixed, and checks exact saved solids at the declared gate angles. The coarse step finds a candidate interval; bisection refines a clear-to-hit bracket. A missed narrow collision window between coarse samples is possible. These are proxy sensitivities, not measured uncertainty, actual-pan bounds or predictions.

@@FIT_SENSITIVITY@@

Can the insert withdraw vertically?

@@FIT_PATH_NOTE@@

The cover is removed and the gates stay closed. Saved geometry is sampled every 1 mm from nominal XY/yaw until the insert clears the pan rim. The alternate route omits the complete pickup/tube proxy as an assembly. This is a sampled digital path, not a continuous sweep proof or verified service procedure.

@@FIT_PATHS@@

What if it shifts or tilts first?

@@ALT_EXIT_NOTE@@

Eight selected routes are tested for each concept: ±20 mm X/Y shifts or ±5° X/Y tilts at installed height, followed by a vertical lift. The cover is removed; gates remain closed; pickup/tube and nominal shell proxies stay fixed. Shifts use 1 mm and tilts 1° samples (tilt rotates about the insert bounding-box centre). These checks can find sampled intersections, but a clear result does not prove continuous clearance, a complete search, or a service procedure.
